Figure 6a. Full-hierarchy parameters versus working-set size. m is processor-specific local-DRAM read events per completed benchmark load from the unsampled trial. h weights the sampled mean L1/L2/L3 load latencies by matched unsampled source counts; p is the sampled DRAM mean minus h. Each point summarizes per-trial arithmetic means using the median and IQR across 30 trials. Timings are effective dispatch-to-completion latencies, including pipeline effects, converted using concurrent reference/core-cycle counts. Hollow markers indicate fewer than 80% of trials meet all support checks, including 1,000 observations per required class. Missing classes are not imputed.

Figure 6b. AMAT reconstructed as h + mp from each paired trial, compared with independently timed pointer-chain access. Bands show trial IQR, not confidence intervals. This is a measured-component reconstruction, not a fitted predictive model. Hardware samples and whole-loop timing have distinct measurement envelopes; disagreement is retained. Vertical lines mark nominal L1 (48 KiB), L2 (1.25 MiB), and L3 (24 MiB) capacities.
